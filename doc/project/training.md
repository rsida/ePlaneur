# Training

Training is one of the core purposes of the club. The approach mirrors real gliding training: learn
step by step, understand what you do, avoid "gaming" (flying without method). Nobody is prevented from
flying before validating a step, but the club recommends following the path.

## Three practice levels

| Level | Content | Simulator |
|---|---|---|
| Initiation | Discover Condor, basic piloting, solo autonomy on a simple circuit, then network flying | Condor 2 |
| Standard | Longer and harder circuits, flaps, MacCready, external flight computer, trace analysis, switch to Condor 3 | Condor 2 → Condor 3 at step 7 |
| Competition | Optional extension for pilots mastering Condor 3; training for the French ePlaneur Championship | Condor 3 |

Training starts on **Condor 2** on purpose: the club can lend Condor 2 licences, it runs on modest
PCs, its weather model and flight computer are simpler, and there is no airspace regulation.

## The 8-step guided path

| # | Level | Step | Internal qualification | Detailed page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initiation | Discover Condor and glider piloting (exercises, not circuits) | **Élève ePilote** | Published |
| 2 | Initiation | Become autonomous solo: short simple circuits, record successful flights | **ePilote Solo** | To publish |
| 3 | Initiation | Learn network flying: short easy circuits, no penalty | **ePilote** | To publish |
| 4 | Standard | Master flaps; pass the Condor Club **Silver badge** tests | **ePilote breveté Condor Club** | To publish |
| 5 | Standard | Fly with MacCready speed-to-fly | — | To publish |
| 6 | Standard | Use an external flight computer (XCSoar) | — | To publish |
| 7 | Standard | Move to Condor 3 | — | To publish |
| 8 | Standard | Prepare the future FFVP Brevet ePlaneur (theory) | — | To publish |

Qualifications are **internal to the club**, not federal diplomas. Each step page describes:
objectives, skills, resources, method and validation criteria. A PDF summary exists for the path
(`Parcours-type-ePlaneur-V03`) and for step 1.

### Step 1 in detail (model for the other steps)

- Goal: use Condor 2 essential functions and reproduce basic piloting exercises (straight line, speed
  holding, medium bank turns, controls effects, instruments, forces balance). Exercises are not
  recorded on the Condor Club.
- Vocabulary: pitch/roll/yaw axes, attitude and bank, adverse yaw, control surfaces, stick and rudder
  coordination, airspeed indicator, variometer, altimeter, compass, joystick/rudder/keyboard commands.
- Resources: FFVP teaching videos (25 videos, 8 playlists, 4 h 11), ePlaneur training videos,
  eplaneur.fr, Condor 2 free flight with exercise flight plans (AA2 and Slovenia sceneries).
- Rudder: automatic coordination, joystick twist, or real pedals (recommended for future real pilots).
- Validation: the club may run a knowledge and skills check before awarding **Élève ePilote**.

## Training videos page

Videos are ordered by step: club presentation; step 1 full solo flight on the FrSW scenery
(Issoire – Sancy – Puy de Dôme); step 2 series on flight plan `C2-d015` (Issoire – Brioude) at 150,
170 and 160 km/h, plus landing at Brioude; step 3 reuses the 150 km/h video (server connection, Stop
Join); steps 4–8 point to documents (MacCready, XCSoar presentation and coupling) until videos exist.

## Progression tracking

Experimental, **not yet validated by the committee**. Today it is an Excel workbook per student on a
shared Google Drive (sheets: Presentation, Condor resources read, FFVP videos watched + exercises
done, Skills validated per step); trainers validate and comment. A first version was used with the
EPITECH students. Tracking progression and qualifications in the application is a natural evolution.

## Brevet ePlaneur project

Long-term project to propose a **Brevet ePlaneur** to the FFVP. It does **not exist** and the FFVP
has taken no decision. Envisaged content:

- Practical part on Condor 3 with recorded traces as verifiable evidence (circuit, distance, duration,
  altitude gain, turnpoints, penalties). The Condor Club Silver badge (1 h flight, 1,000 m gain, 50 km
  straight distance) is the current reference.
- Theoretical part to define with the FFVP (principles of flight, instruments, meteorology,
  navigation, airspace, flight preparation, human factors, safety), possibly based on the SPL theory.
- Transparent, identical criteria for all candidates, and traceability of results.
- Roadmap: finish the 8 steps, define a skills framework, build reproducible tests, experiment,
  analyse, submit a file to the FFVP.

## Good practices content

The site insists on: alternating structured learning and free flights, following the 8 steps, using
the teaching resources, joining workshops and supervised network flights, setting precise goals.
Getting started requires: an adequate PC, a joystick, a Condor licence, a stable Internet connection,
Discord + headset, a Condor Club account, optionally remote assistance (the club has an Iperius Remote
licence) and WhatsApp on the PC. A full installation takes several hours, sometimes days.
