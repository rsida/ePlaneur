# Network flights

Network flights are multiplayer Condor sessions: every pilot connects from home to the same server
and flies the same task with the same weather. They are the club's main recurring activity, open to
the public (registration on the site required, free), as pilot or spectator. History is kept since
2020.

## Levels and weekly schedule

Times are always **metropolitan France time** (CET/CEST). La Réunion: +2 h in summer, +3 h in winter.

(*) Every other week the Condor 3 evening slots alternate between Standard and Competition; before the
French Championship, competition training has priority. Schedule as of 2026-03.

| Level | Purpose | Characteristics | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initiation (C2) | Learn how a multiplayer session works, with a coach | Short easy circuits, generous weather, goal = finish, no ranking | 45 min – 1 h 15 |
| Standard (C2/C3) | Core activity, regular practice | Varied circuits, max 2 h sessions | 1 h 15 – 2 h |
| Competition (C3) | Prepare for the French Championship | Briefing at 20:45, regatta starts, demanding tasks | 2 – 3 h |

**Summer break**: from about May to mid-October, a reduced service runs mostly on existing flight
plans; a "Pause estivale" banner is displayed on both sites' home pages.

## Taking part

Prerequisites: Condor, scenery and hangar versions must match the server exactly, otherwise the
connection is refused (versions are listed on the prerequisites page, recent updates highlighted in
red). Reference versions in 2026: Condor 2.2.0 / hangar 25 / AA2 1.00; Condor 3.0.9 / hangar 43 /
AA3 0.6; FrSW scenery for both.

Sequence:

1. Join Discord server "ePlaneur", voice channel "AU SOL : Briefing", then the channel of the flight
   (Initiation C2, Standard C2, Standard C3, Competition).
2. Connect to the Condor server (FFVP "AuvRAlpes" servers, with backup servers; an `AddressBook.ini`
   file is provided), Join, choose and ballast the glider, Join again.
3. **Stop Join** window (5–20 min) to connect or reconnect, then 5–10 min before the task opens.
4. Start: classic start (about 15 min to cross the line, last start counts) or **regatta start**
   (everyone at the gate opening; systematic in competition training).
5. Fly the task; live tracking sometimes on Cunimb-Condor.
6. Record the performance: upload the **FTR** trace to the **Condor Club circuit** whose link was
   posted in the WhatsApp group (not via the logbook, which can pick the wrong circuit variant).
7. Results table and sometimes trace analyses (SportsTrackLive) are shared on WhatsApp.

## Rules

- Respect the ballast limit (Condor 2: Condor Club penalty of 2 min per extra kg).
- Circle **left only** from take-off until the start and during the first 10 km of the task.
- Cross the start below **170 km/h ground speed** (penalty 3 s per km/h above in Condor 2).
- Respect right-of-way priorities as in real flight.
- Stay within gliding range of an airfield or landable field.
- Regatta start (from the 2021 virtual Grand Prix): no going back after the start (disqualification),
  early start penalty = 10 × the anticipation time, 5 km start line, be on the right side of the
  extended line 1 min before.

## Organising a flight

Any member can propose a flight. Requirements: a named organiser (no organiser, no flight); date and
time agreed with participants and consistent with usual slots; a flight plan suited to the level
(from the FPL database, FPL sheets, Condor Club circuits); Stop Join duration; server availability
checked with the server manager; Condor/hangar/glider consistency; announcement in the WhatsApp group
(date, time, FPL code, Condor Club circuit link) well in advance. During the session the organiser
runs the Discord briefing 15 min before, coordinates, and reminds pilots to record their performance.

Preparing a brand-new flight is heavy: design the circuit, test it, add it to the database, create or
check the Condor Club circuit and FPL sheet, publish on both sites.

## Flight data and tools

Flight plans (FPL) and sessions are stored in a database on **eplaneur.fr** with a normalised coding
(FPL codes such as `C2-d015`). The tools are hosted on eplaneur.fr and publicly reachable; the club
site documents each of them (the membership pages present access to "ePlaneur tools" as a member
benefit):

| Tool | Function |
|---|---|
| Search FPL | Multi-criteria search (Condor version, flight type, scenery, glider, departure airfield, distance, difficulty) |
| Show FPL | All FPL data, download `.fpl` (Condor) and `.tsk` (XCSoar), FPL sheet, Condor Club circuit |
| Show sessions | Session history filtered by year, Condor version, FPL category, dedicated/open; includes competitions (French Championship 2024, FAI eSport 2025) |
| Session results | Results table of a session, previous/next navigation |
| Last session per FPL | Helps members re-propose popular flights |

**FPL sheet** (fiche FPL): summary of a flight (plan, circuit, glider, conditions) with a VAC chart for
the final landing; all initiation flights and most Condor 2 standard flights have one, Condor 3 and
competition flights generally do not.
