## Open questions

- Flight tracks: the task time of C3-c060 is 63 s shorter than the Condor result (4,846 s against
  1 h 21 min 49 s on the server): its `.fpl` gives a regatta start (window 0), so the official time
  counts from the opening of the gate; the flight page shows the official time of the results. And a flight with a crash in the middle
  (C3-c058: 500 m higher, then a fast fall) keeps only one of its parts: how should it be shown?
- Clouds in 3D (7k4), to check on a test flight with the `.fpl` of C3-c060 or C3-s117 (in
  `Documents\Condor3\RaceResults`): the Flight Planner gives the cloud base above the ground ("1900m
  agl", "2000m agl"), and the thermal tops of the stored flights stay under the inversion taken
  above sea level (C3-s117: tops 2,467 m at most for an inversion at 2,625 m, 600 to 900 m above
  the ground). The model therefore expects blue thermals over the Alps on C3-s117 (ground near
  1,400 m, base near 3,400 m) and cumulus over the low ground on C3-c060. The planner sketch draws
  the ground at 0 m and always shows a cloud: look at the sky in flight to confirm. The test flight
  of 2026-10-12 on C3-c060 (`var/condor-study/test/`) contradicts the model: Condor counted 1 point
  of cloud flying (200 a minute, so 0.3 s in cloud) and its UDP log the cloud entered at 2,835 m,
  at the top of a thermal above the inversion (2,794 m), where the model puts a blue thermal
  (lowest ground under it 1,195 m, base 3,076 m). A base of 2,835 m is 1,881 m above 954 m, about
  the mean ground within 8 to 10 km of the thermal (971 and 949 m; 952 m within 10 km with the
  terrain of Condor itself, read from the landscape file, see condor-integration):
  Condor may take the base above a smoothed ground, and let clouds form a little above the
  inversion. One point only: more thermals topped in cloud are needed before changing the model.
- Sessions (9f): results and pilot names are public on eplaneur.fr; on the club site they need an
  account. Should they be public? Should the session menu items (Afficher sessions, Résultats session)
  point to `/sessions` now?

- Teacher space (11f): must a teacher be a member of the club, and who grants `CLASS_TEACH`? Consent of

- Messages (9c): should a member be able to report a message, and who would read the reports? Should

- Gliders: the polars of Condor do not say at which mass they are given; taken without water at the
