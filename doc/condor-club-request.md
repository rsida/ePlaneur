# Request to the Condor Club team (draft)

Draft of the message to the authors of Condor Club (roadmap step 7n2), to be reviewed and sent by
the club. Placeholders in brackets. Written in English, Condor Club's language; a French version
can be made if they prefer it.

---

**Subject:** Club ePlaneur: linking our training site with Condor Club

Hello,

I am [first name, last name], member of the board of the Club ePlaneur ([club.eplaneur.fr](https://club.eplaneur.fr)),
a French association that teaches gliding on Condor. The club runs entirely online: [number] members,
an 8-step training path from the first flight to cross-country, and weekly network flights on our
server, most of them listed on Condor Club.

We are building a new site for the club, mainly for training: we want to attract younger pilots, and
they expect modern debriefing tools in one place. We do not want to duplicate what Condor Club does
well. Your logbook, circuits, rankings and competitions stay the reference, and we encourage our
members to use them and to take a Premium subscription.

What our site adds is on the training side: an instructor can comment a flight at a given moment,
a flight is debriefed step by step against the skills of our training path, and a whole session
can be replayed together in a club debriefing evening.

To do this without asking our members for one more account or upload, we would like to link both
sites for our club sessions:

1. **Create the circuit on Condor Club** from our site when the club plans a session (flight plan,
   date, server), so that it appears on Condor Club as it does today when we create it by hand.
2. **Share the tracks of a session** both ways: a flight uploaded on Condor Club could be read by our
   site for the debriefing, and a flight uploaded on our site could be sent to Condor Club so that
   it counts in the pilot's logbook.

Today our site only reads the public data of a circuit page from its link (`/api/task/<id>`, with a
`User-Agent` naming our site), to show the settings of a session. We have not tried to go further on
our own, and we would not do it without your agreement.

Would you be open to this? If you are, we would follow whatever suits you: an access key for the
club, a documented endpoint, limits on the number of requests, or another way you would prefer. If
you would rather not, we will simply keep linking to Condor Club pages, as we do now.

We would also be glad to share what we learn about training, if any of it could be useful to
Condor Club.

Thank you for Condor Club and for the time you give to the Condor community.

Best regards,
